📷 Scan QR Code

Use your webcam. Hold the QR code in front of the camera, then press Capture.

1. Decode

OpenCV + pyzbar read the QR image.

2. Analyze

HTTPS, shorteners, IPs, keywords and domain tricks are checked.

3. Report

You get a risk score and clear advice.

The score is a heuristic (rule-based estimate). It is not a guarantee that a website is safe.
